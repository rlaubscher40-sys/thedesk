# The Desk: owner decisions and lawyer brief

Prepared 14 September 2026. Draft instructions for review. Not signed, sent or filed.

## Business description

The Desk (thedesk.au) is an Australian property intelligence and publishing product. It collects third-party reporting and public data, creates AI-assisted articles and answers, distributes email briefings and publishes social graphics and narrated Reels. Code is in rlaubscher40-sys/thedesk. Ruben Laubscher directs the project. His editorial role is not proof of legal ownership of every asset.

## Draft founder IP assignment instructions

Prepare an assignment between the verified current owner(s) and the intended company, once it exists and the chain of title is established.

Schedule the assets explicitly: source code and commit history; original designs and copy; original audiovisual works; domains and trade marks; owned database structure/compilations where rights subsist; confidential workflows and documentation. Identify third-party material and existing licences as exclusions. Do not purport to assign public facts, someone else's data, or copyright that does not subsist.

Cover existing rights and appropriate future rights, further-assurance obligations, consideration/tax review, delivery of files and credentials, retained background IP licences, and existing encumbrances. Address moral rights with legally appropriate specific consents, not an assertion that moral rights are transferable. Avoid absolute ownership warranties unsupported by the evidence, particularly for AI output and legacy MIT-labelled code.

## Draft contributor agreement instructions

Before future work begins, use a written scope and agreement identifying the parties, deliverables, fees and timing. Specify the ownership/assignment of project IP, treatment of background IP, a sufficient licence to use necessary background material, and delivery of editable/source files. Require disclosure and approval of third-party materials and their licences. Cover confidentiality, permitted AI use, prohibited submission of confidential data to unapproved tools, attribution/moral-rights consents, security, access return, and termination handover.

Have the lawyer distinguish employees from contractors and tailor the terms. An NDA alone does not transfer IP. Do not paste this term sheet into a signature flow as though it were an executed agreement.

## Draft employment exclusion instructions

If necessary after reviewing the actual agreement, seek an express written acknowledgement identifying The Desk, its domain/repository, pre-existing and independently developed assets, permitted outside-business activity, resource separation, confidentiality obligations and handling of conflicts. The proposed acknowledgement must accurately state when/how work was created. Do not claim an exclusion was agreed or that an employer has no rights without evidence.

## Rights review worksheet

For each source or asset retain: asset/source ID; exact source URL and creator; relevant licence/terms URL; dated copy of permission evidence; permitted collection, AI processing, adaptation and commercial redistribution; territories/platforms/duration; attribution and change notices; endorsement/personality concerns; restrictions; reviewer; approval status; review trigger; and every published item using it.

The generated register supplies starting records, not permission. In particular, a robots.txt allowance controls crawling etiquette/access only; a free webpage, attribution or an RSS feed is not blanket permission for reuse.

## Questions for insurance broker

Does cover expressly include AI-assisted editorial work, website/email/social publishing, copyright and defamation claims, defence costs and overseas claims? What exclusions apply to contractual liability, media activities, financial advice, known circumstances and retrospective publications? Confirm cyber incident response and privacy-event support. Do not assume an ordinary business policy covers this operation.
