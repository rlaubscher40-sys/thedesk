## Disclosure copy for future use

Use only when factually accurate and with actual names/arrangements filled in:

- Sponsorship: “Advertisement. Paid for by [sponsor].” Place prominently before the promotion, not only among hashtags.
- Referral: “If you use this link and become a client of [provider], The Desk may receive [accurate description of payment].” Show before the reader acts.
- Relevant relationship: “Disclosure: [accurate relationship between the publisher/author and the subject].” Describe the relationship concretely.

Do not publish bracketed placeholders or invent a sponsor. The feed triage catches some explicit commercial phrases but does not implement a sponsorship product or detect undisclosed arrangements. Social promotional claims must be accurate and supportable; monitor misleading content on the business's pages. [ACCC guidance](https://www.accc.gov.au/business/advertising-and-promotions/social-media-promotions).
