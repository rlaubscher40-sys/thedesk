# Free property data review — 9 September 2026

## Domain auction results

The public results page loaded without an API subscription on 9 September 2026:
https://www.domain.com.au/auction-results/

Domain's help article describes six months of weekly results for Sydney,
Melbourne, Brisbane, Adelaide and Canberra. Perth, Hobart and Darwin are
excluded. These are capital-city observations, not statewide coverage:
https://help.domain.com.au/hc/en-au/articles/360016343254-View-Domain-auction-results

Markets now links readers directly to that public page and states the coverage.
The link does not fetch, cache or import Domain data and introduces no API or LLM
calls. It does not populate the state or Australian metrics.

Conditions of Use section 3.1(b)(ix–x) restrict extraction and building a property
database. Public visibility and the weekly email option do not establish reuse
permission. Do not enable collection from this page until suitable permission is
confirmed:
https://www.domain.com.au/group/conditions-of-use/

The free-access enquiry has been acknowledged under Domain ticket #3019604;
permission and free production access have not been confirmed. No purchase or
paid trial was authorised. PropTrack/REA remains excluded.

## Victoria rental data

The official rental report and DataVic catalogue still identify September 2025
as the latest quarterly release checked on 9 September 2026:
https://www.dffh.vic.gov.au/publications/rental-report
https://discover.data.vic.gov.au/dataset/rental-report-quarterly-quarterly-median-rents-by-lga

The catalogue specifies CC BY 4.0. The linked XLSX is a potential historical
baseline, but ordinary downloads in this workspace timed out; its contents have
not been validated or imported. No Victoria collector has been enabled. Do not
label the September 2025 release as current simply because it is downloaded now.

The next useful step is obtaining and validating the official workbook, then
implementing a parser against its actual geography, categories, reporting dates
and suppression rules. No invented fixture or headline median should stand in
for that validation.
