# Reel quality and audience learning

Implemented September 2026. Admin → Instagram → Automatic narrated Reels → **Reel quality, audience learning and production buffer**.

## Before the next production batch

1. Check the documentary buffer. Begin research when fewer than four registered, unpublished exports retain eligible dates, or the last date is less than fourteen days away. Resolve unknown publication receipts before counting stock. Missed release dates do not authorise backfill.
2. Read the previous comparable films' review notes and the recorded next test. Make one purposeful change: opening stakes, an explanatory picture, or delivery. Keep the approved voice settings unless an actual audition supports a revision.
3. A new documentary still requires verified claims, a person-led progression, meaningful and cleared pictures, and a supported payoff. The research scaffold and renderer do not substitute for that editorial work.
4. Run `pnpm documentary:review` with its documented arguments. Each future package includes `review.html` beside the MP4: full-video playback, scene jump buttons, the ten-part editorial rubric, timestamped notes and an exact-export JSON review download. Keep the folder together. This local record does not silently approve, publish or sync itself to the admin database.
5. Complete a full watch and listen. Inspect names, figures, breathing room, mobile captions, explanatory visuals and the ending. Technical diagnostics are listening prompts; a target pace or populated checklist does not prove creative quality.

## After publication

The operations report joins at most 80 recent confirmed publication receipts to their exact saved source/export, Instagram media ID, immutable first-day measurement and human review. Missing historical provenance stays missing. The published video hash, voice engine and actual recorded delivery diagnostics are visible next to the review.

Record the full-watch/full-listen attestation, six review criteria, timestamped fixes and one next test. Reviews are tied to the publication key, period, media ID and submitted video SHA-256. Admin identity comes from the authenticated session. A conditional revision prevents two tabs silently overwriting each other. These notes cannot alter publication locks, release dates or existing videos.

## Learn without inventing a winner

The existing twice-daily insights job collects engagement counts. The report groups usable first-day observations by editorial family, recipe, voice identity/speed, recorded delivery profile, 15-second duration band and six-hour observation-age band within 24–48 hours. Old exports with a recorded voice but no delivery diagnostics have their own `delivery-unrecorded` cohort.

Median saves and shares per 1,000 reached are calculated per post, with a separate sample count for each metric. Missing values are not zeros. Missing or zero reach, out-of-window readings and absent export provenance are excluded and counted. Late retries do not replace an earlier coherent first-day snapshot.

Five usable observations per metric is an operating minimum for selecting a test, **not** statistical significance. Compare stories within a cohort, inspect the actual films and record a single-variable hypothesis. Do not infer a voice winner, change rotation or make causal claims from these observational differences. Topic, audience and distribution remain confounders.

Watch-time, completion and retention are not collected by this report. Reach, saves and shares cannot stand in for them. Adding supported provider watch metrics and accumulating comparable audience evidence remain separate work; no historical retention values are manufactured.

## Unchanged release behaviour

This release adds reporting and review tools. Existing authorised archival MP4s, narration defaults, captions, publication identities, scheduling, family rotation and duplicate guards remain unchanged. Registered archival approval is displayed separately from a newly recorded human review.
