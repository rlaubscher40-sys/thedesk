# Regional evidence follow-through · 17 September 2026

## Delivered change

Public market files expose an exact matching Queensland RTA LGA rent snapshot, rather than requiring the reader to discover it through a separate search. One optional, bounded database read serves the directory. It never substitutes the namesake suburb, NSW postcode or state for a missing council. Observations retain the stored quarter, publisher category, suppressed values, bond-count caveat, source URL and retrieval date. Historic links pin the source's council and quarter. This reuses the existing validated collector; it is not a new data acquisition or a fresh-download claim.

Townsville and Newcastle also receive two reviewed primary-source context notes each. They are rendered identically for the server and client, separately from the automatic news counts and confidence/indexing rules. They do not change Ask's evidence, automatically post to social/email, or pretend a government/housing-operator statement is independent verification. Source review dates remain fixed; a review-due warning appears after 30 days. Any new or updated note requires original-source review and a tested code change.
