# Housing Reel: audience review

## Ready to compare

The question opening asks why buying is harder while homes are being built.
The consequence opening introduces the longer climb to a deposit. The two
review exports share every subsequent scene, phrase, visual and source. Their
opening durations may differ because Fable keeps its natural speed.

Run `node --import tsx scripts/review-housing-balance-reel.ts --out /absolute/path`
for the question opening. Add `--opening consequence` for the alternative.
The production candidate retains the question opening until a separate decision
changes it. Both share the same publication identity to prevent duplicate
automatic posting. These review commands never publish.

## One-view comprehension check

Show one version once to 3 to 5 Australian property readers, including people
saving a deposit. Do not explain the story before the viewing. Ask:

1. What happened to housing supply compared with new need?
2. Why does that matter to buyers or renters?
3. What needs to happen to start closing the shortage?

Record the viewer's own words and any scene they could not follow. A successful
explanation should communicate insufficient net additions, competition and
upward pressure, and the need for additions to outpace additional need. If a
viewer hears guaranteed price increases, 55,000 people priced out, or believes
the decade-long deposit comparison was caused by this 18-month flow gap, revise
that transition. This small check is qualitative feedback, not proof of reach.

## Opening comparison after publication is authorised

Compare opening variants with the body and caption held constant. Avoid changing
the topic, thumbnail, audience and opening together. Separate organic posts are
not a randomised experiment; timing and distribution may explain differences.

Record the observation window, reach, available retention curve and average
watch time. Calculate shares and saves per account reached and follows per reach
where those metrics exist. Use completion only if provided reliably. Inspect
comments for understanding as well as interest. Do not infer comprehension
from likes or declare a winner from a handful of views.

Compare several similar episodes and comparable observation windows before
changing the default. Do not set a universal retention target or claim that
these edits establish parity with Glasshouse's audience performance.

## Editorial continuity

The revised facts scene removes the repeated national introduction. It names
homes added, homes needed and the additional gap in three measured phrases.
The uncovered gold segment retains its scale as it moves into the pressure
scene, replacing the generic people-and-house drawing. A larger central phrase
changes the composition at the explanatory turning point. The separate deposit
benchmark keeps its years, saving assumption and modelled status visible.
Source names remain complete and their references are larger. The caption now
introduces the buyer consequence before its modelling details.
