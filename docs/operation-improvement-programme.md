## Boundaries

No paid data subscriptions, new vendor purchase, weakening of source-access restrictions, reset of social publication locks, fabricated social proof or automatic approval of unreviewed documentary assets. Existing manual-release preference and declined GitHub branch protection are retained. Public content and operational updates are part of this programme; direct messages, campaigns to people and production test emails require explicit recipient/action authorization.

Audience growth, subscriber retention, revenue, inbox placement, legal ownership and a real backup restore require observed outcomes or access beyond code. They remain open until evidence exists. A skipped low-value post is acceptable; an unexplained silent gap is not.

## Editorial and distribution programme

### Reader promise

Help people buying, owning or following Australian property understand what changed, what the evidence supports, and what remains unknown. The morning briefing is the short entry point; Markets and Data provide depth; Ask resolves a specific question. A professional occupation is not a prerequisite.

### Weekly commissioning plan

| Output                       | Reader job                                                     | Commissioning rule                                                                                                                     | Review                                                                                         |
| ---------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Weekday briefing             | Understand the important property developments by 7am Sydney   | Prefer consequential original reporting/releases; rank by decision relevance, not publisher volume; hold a weak day rather than pad it | Check headline, excerpt, causal claim, dates, source and reader angle before distribution      |
| Weekly analysis              | Connect the week's developments                                | One defensible thesis, contrary evidence, what would falsify it; no imposed contrarian conclusion                                      | Lead and closing paragraph must be supported by the cited material                             |

| Property Empires documentary | Explain a consequential person/company through verified scenes | Use the existing approved production standard, rights record and evidence pack                                                         | New creative approval is required; no template substitution for scene/voice review             |
| Market file                  | Research a place                                               | Prefer local housing evidence; show reporting age, matched measures and explicit gaps                                                  | Thin evidence cannot be relabelled as deep coverage                                            |

Daily automated availability is conditional. Fifteen data recipes are not fifteen fresh stories every day. A consumed source period stays consumed even after a revision. Do not reset publication identities to maintain frequency. Evaluate whether city variants become repetitive; stop expanding the template if the reader outcome is weak.

### Four-week experiment sequence after release

1. **Days 1–7: accuracy and delivery.** Verify the three website corrections, the exact July Brisbane/Perth rent answer, the missing sentiment arrow, corrections links, newsletter recovery states, external probe results and Reel outcomes. Inspect each newly published item's source and its matched destination. Freeze creative/frequency changes while defects are being resolved.
2. **Days 8–14: opening clarity.** Compare a plain finding-led opening with a misconception-led opening on similar data subjects. Change only the opening. Read platform results at the same 48-hour age; record reach, views, saves and shares separately. Do not call sequential posts a randomized A/B test.
3. **Days 15–21: useful next action.** Compare “read the matched data” with a save/share prompt on comparable posts, while keeping the destination and format stable. Inspect tagged website arrivals and onward/source actions. Bio traffic is shared and cannot identify the originating post.
4. **Days 22–28: retention and focus.** Review confirmed acquisition, unsubscribe counts, reading actions and the most useful topics. Retain the stronger editorial approach only if the evidence is consistent across several comparable releases. With low samples, record “inconclusive” and continue the same bounded experiment; do not scale output to compensate for weak usefulness.

No paid acquisition, engagement purchase, automated outreach, unsolicited DMs or newsletter campaign is authorized by this plan. New campaigns require a specific audience, content and send authorization. This is a commissioning/measurement plan, not evidence that future experiments have run.

### Measurement sheet specification

One row per confirmed publication: platform post ID; source period; topic/family; opening variant; actual publish time; time of insight capture; insight availability; reach; views; saves; shares; profile visits if available; tagged destination; correction/incident flag. Keep unavailable values blank with a reason, not zero. Report shares/reach and saves/reach only when reach is available and nonzero. Compare equal observation ages. At the operation level report tagged arrivals, onward reads, source opens, confirmed subscriptions by acquisition channel, and unsubscribes separately. These are not one attributable funnel unless a real joined denominator exists.

Starting evidence from the 16 September audit: Instagram 214 posts, 11 followers; the inspected 30-day account view showed 1,743 views, 139 viewers, seven engaged accounts and seven profile visits; six confirmed email subscribers. These are dated observations from different surfaces, not comparable conversion denominators. Archive the next comparable observation before claiming improvement.

## Recovery and cost checks

- Record a failed health probe independently of whether its history can be saved. A successful recording request never makes an unhealthy application healthy. Five-minute cron is a requested cadence, not a guaranteed service level; check sample coverage and workflow notifications.
- Before a restore exercise, document provider backup age/retention and the target recovery point/time. Restore into a private isolated environment with publishing, schedulers and outbound email disabled. Check schema, story counts, publication locks, consent/unsubscribe state and representative reads. Never test by overwriting production. The signed result must record backup timestamp, restored timestamp, elapsed time, discrepancies and responsible operator.
- For rollback, revert the release commit through a new reviewed commit and deploy the previous known-good code. Source corrections are deliberate database edits and do not revert with code; never restore known-wrong copy merely to match an old revision. No schema migration is introduced by this release. Confirm `/api/healthz`, public read routes and scheduler/publication states after rollback.
- Reconcile monthly Railway, model and email invoices. Inspect render durations/failures and source transfer metrics already present in admin. Keep local speech and existing source caches; reuse exact verified facts where a model is unnecessary. This release reduces redundant daily-brief expiry writes and removes a model dependency from bounded CPI rent lookups. No savings amount is claimed without measurement.

## Remaining external evidence

The code can expose and reduce risk; it cannot manufacture provider access, historical platform corrections, rights permissions, inbox placement, an executed restore, audience growth or revenue. For each, keep a dated record with owner, evidence needed, next action and result. An unavailable item remains open and is not deducted as an invented zero or promoted to a pass.

### Reader journey and measurement follow-through · 16 September 2026

This patch closes additional observed gaps rather than asserting audience growth:

- A story is marked opened only after its record loads. Missing, held, failed and malformed story routes cannot clear its unread state. Successful load events deduplicate refetches and StrictMode effects.
- Original-reporting clicks on Today, related coverage, Story and Saved are measured with fixed surface labels. Story-to-Ask navigation and completed link copies/native shares are also recorded; cancelled or failed sharing is not counted as success.
- Ask submissions are separated from returned answers, no-answer responses and request failures. A returned answer does not prove accuracy. Restoring an existing answer does not create a new outcome event.
- An accepted newsletter request is measured without an email address or subscriber identifier. It is not a confirmed subscription, delivery receipt or inbox-placement result. Honeypot requests are excluded; the existing private subscriber report remains the source for confirmed subscriptions.
- Browser telemetry falls back when Beacon refuses its payload; failed transports cannot interrupt the reader's action. Referrer URLs are reduced to hostnames before transmission and revalidated on the server. Do Not Track, ephemeral tab sessions and blocked-storage behaviour are retained. No persistent visitor identifier or additional vendor is introduced.
- The private seven-day report counts sessions with a page view and each observed action inside the same window. It aggregates in SQL, excludes unmatched action-only sessions and out-of-window rows, and counts repeated actions once per session. Counts overlap and are not a sequential funnel, cross-device reach or returning-reader measurement.
- Missing database access produces unavailable/error states rather than fabricated zero readership. New reader-action history starts at deployment; it cannot reconstruct the past. Platform insights, subscriber records and website sessions must not be divided into a claimed conversion rate without matching evidence.

The exact-head release PR records CI, deployment and live verification. No new account, paid upgrade, outbound message, production test subscription, publication or schema migration is part of this patch.

### Crash diagnostic privacy and delivery · 16 September 2026

PR295 merged and deployed as `99bb1ad828a8f719451105fb72b52d065de37937`. All 2,656 tests and release gates passed; six read-only live health/access checks passed.

Further failure-path review found that automatic browser errors and startup-loop reports included full page URLs in their location and stack fields. Those can contain an Ask question or a signed link. A shared diagnostic boundary now strips HTTP URL credentials, queries and fragments, including URL references inside messages/stacks and relative request URLs. It runs before browser transmission and again before server persistence. Asset paths and normal stack line/column locations remain useful for diagnosis. This bounded URL scrubber does not claim to detect arbitrary personal data in free-form error text, and it does not rewrite historic stored reports.

Messages and stacks are bounded to the existing storage sizes, so ordinary long errors no longer fail the API's old length limits. The API retains explicit upper input bounds for older clients. Browser reports deduplicate after sanitisation and are capped at ten attempts per minute per loaded document; a burst beyond the cap is intentionally dropped until the window resets. Reporter initialisation is idempotent, and transport or thrown-object conversion failures cannot turn explicit reporting into a new crash. Startup diagnostics also remove URL extras at construction.

Tests inspect the actual outbound payloads, server persistence inputs, global error/rejection handlers, burst limits, duplicate registration, long errors and transport failures using synthetic values.
