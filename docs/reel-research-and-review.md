# Housing Reel: research, direction and review

Reviewed 9 September 2026. This is a record of design decisions, not a claim of
measured audience performance or a numerical quality score.

## References actually examined

- Mike Bostock, [Object Constancy](https://bost.ocks.org/mike/constancy/).
  Keeping a data element identifiable through a transition makes changes easier
  to follow. Applied by retaining the supply row and endpoint while need and the
  gap arrive. Motion follows the explanation rather than introducing new layouts.
- Financial Times Visual Journalism Team,
  [Visual Vocabulary](https://github.com/Financial-Times/chart-doctor/blob/main/visual-vocabulary/README.md).
  The relationship determines the graphic. Applied through paired bars for
  magnitude and a 100-dot display for a whole-number ratio. Quantitative bars
  share a zero baseline and a 300,000-home scale. The ending is labelled as an
  illustration and carries no forecast amounts.
- The Pudding, [Film Dialogue](https://pudding.cool/2017/03/film-dialogue/).
  The published piece starts with a recognisable subset before broadening its
  analysis, with methodology available alongside it. Our adaptation is to
  introduce one side of the housing comparison before adding the other, then
  explain the implication. This is an editorial inference, not evidence about
  Instagram retention or a reproduction of that interactive article.
- Nielsen Norman Group,
  [The Role of Animation and Motion in UX](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/animation-purpose-ux/).
  Its UI guidance warns that concurrent motion competes for attention. Our
  adaptation is one principal changing element at a time and deliberate holds.
  This is an inference for film direction, not a tested short-video recipe.

- School of Motion, [Illustration skills for motion design](https://schoolofmotion.com/blog/illustration-skills-motion-design).
  Read the sections on reducing an idea, testing rough scenes and evaluating an
  animatic without sound. Applied to the key-and-door metaphor and the seven
  frame sequence. No claim to have watched its embedded course is made.
- School of Motion, [Motion design demo reels](https://schoolofmotion.com/blog/top-tips-tricks-killer-motion-design-demo-reel).
  Its opening, repetition and reading-time advice applies as a craft heuristic;
  this is a portfolio-reel guide, not a controlled Instagram study. Applied by
  moving the contradiction into the first two short utterances, removing the
  standalone evidence scene and keeping completed chart holds.
- Reuters Institute's [interview with NYT visual investigator Malachy Browne](https://reutersinstitute.politics.ox.ac.uk/news/new-york-timess-malachy-browne-future-visual-investigations-age-ai).
  Read the first-person explanation of integrating evidence with the questions
  it answers, while publishing extra methodology separately. Applied through
  the source footer and a visibly derived gap, with full definitions on the
  public read. No unsupported documentary footage is used as evidence.
- PRX/Transom, [WBUR's audio-essay production method](https://transom.org/2026/deploy-this-one-tiny-maneuver-to-improve-audio-essays/).
  The transcript describes Cloe Axelson's move from condensed print prose to
  scripts informed by spoken voice memos and table reads. Applied by simplifying
  short lines and synthesising complete sentences where timing permits. This
  does not make synthetic speech equivalent to a human narrator's performance.
- PRX/Transom, [Remember to Breathe](https://transom.org/2026/revisiting-remember-to-breathe/).
  Read the illustrated edit tutorial and transcript. Retain natural space and
  speech interiors; shorten writing rather than squeezing the delivery. Applied
  by removing needless phrase joins and retaining Fable's normal speed. We do
  not insert someone else's breaths or claim to have heard the linked examples.
- PRX/Transom, [Silence Is Worth Your Time](https://transom.org/2026/silence-is-worth-your-time/).
  Read the Unexplainable creator interview and production transcript. It shows
  pauses being designed around a story. Our adaptation is short, bounded holds;
  its long-form silence experiment is not a recommendation for long dead air
  in this Reel. No decorative music is added to conceal pacing problems.
- PRX/Transom, [Start Your Story](https://transom.org/2016/mark-get-set-start-story/).
  The written breakdown of Offshore distinguishes an opening event, a reason to
  care, a central question and a surprising contrast. Applied by stating the
  housing contradiction early and ending with its explanation. We do not invent
  a real character or anecdote where the reporting supplies none.
- PRX/Transom, [Producing science stories with a scientist](https://transom.org/2015/producing-science-stories-with-a-scientist/).
  Kerry Donahue's production account emphasises general-audience explanations
  and spoken language. Applied by saying homes added and extra homes needed,
  leaving technical model definitions in the source read.
- Journalist Sophia Smith Galer, [her response to the 2024 Digital News Report](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/sophiasmithgaler_if-you-arent-amplifying-your-journalism-activity-7209109656902746112-97C7).
  Read her first-person discussion of platform-specific short video and visible
  evidence. This is creator commentary, not a current audience benchmark for
  The Desk, and its reported percentages are not used to predict our reach.
- YouTube, [Audience retention documentation](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/9314415).
  Retention dips and rewatch spikes need interpretation; a spike can also mean
  confusion. Applied to the proposed post-publication review. These particular
  highlighted reports have platform, length and view requirements, so their
  availability is not promised for this sub-minute Instagram Reel.

Glasshouse's supplied screenshots and Ruben's description remain the visual
reference. Direct playback of the linked Reel was unavailable. No exact timing,
voice comparison or claim to have watched that Reel is made.

## Review boundaries

The expanded research includes fourteen relevant written guides, primary
creator accounts, examples and official documentation. Homepage visits, failed
fetches and tangential film/3D links are not counted as supporting references.
Video playback and embedded audio examples were not directly assessed. This is
not a frame-by-frame audiovisual benchmark against Glasshouse.

The opening, source treatment, chart continuity, human illustration and caption
were assessed visually from the export. Audio presence, timing and levels can
be checked technically here; a listening judgement by the user still matters.
Do not convert technical checks into a claim of perfect narration or guaranteed
retention. Keep the user-approved Fable voice unless a better audition is
actually reviewed.

## Editorial correction, 10 September 2026

The earlier cut still treated the numerical finding as its takeaway. The new
version answers why affordability remains under pressure. The reviewed order
is audience question, net additions, extra need, gap, competition and price
pressure, human consequence, construction constraints, and supply catching up.
The ratio is retained in the source read instead of taking another Reel scene.

The source check confirmed that NHSAC directly links weak supply relative to
demand to price pressure, while also identifying the effect of interest rates
on spending power. That supports a conditional explanation, not an inevitable
price rise. A structured four-beat brief now maps finding, explanation,
consequence and takeaway to evidence and actual scenes before rendering.

The 36.40-second export checks above refer to the earlier cut. New export
measurements are recorded below.

## Connected documentary cut, 10 September 2026

The five remaining production gaps drove this revision: the long numerical
setup, generic imagery, isolated-slide transitions, an abstract human
consequence, and narration phrasing. The eight-scene cut is superseded by six
sequences. Supply, need and shortfall develop within one composition, timed to
three recorded utterances. The shared gold marker moves into the competition
graphic, then becomes the extension of a deposit-saving ruler.

The new human measure is independently grounded in the same pinned report:
9.0 years in 2015 versus 11.2 years in 2025 to save a modelled 20% deposit.
Printed pages 3, 54 and 57 provide the pair, assumptions and current-year detail.
Saving 15% of gross median household income annually is visible on screen and
in the caption/source read. The decade-long comparison is explicitly separate
from the 18-month housing flow. It is not an observed wait, required minimum
deposit or measured consequence caused solely by that flow gap.

Damon Hall's Sydney construction photograph, published in December 2019, now
provides contextual archive imagery in the opening and construction passage.
The Unsplash source and commercial-use licence were checked and recorded in
`reel-archive-photography.md`. Publication year and credit remain visible. This
is not current footage or evidence of delay at the photographed building.

Fable remains at speed 1.0. Eleven complete authored utterances preserve natural
sentence boundaries. The measured phrase audit places each animation phase
against its actual recorded passage. The final deposit number has a reading
hold after its count-up. No speed-up or guessed word timing is used.

The six complete compositions were inspected at phone size. A technical audio
review can establish presence, timing, headroom and decoding, but does not
establish natural vocal performance. No direct listening or Glasshouse playback
comparison was available in this environment. A viewer listening to the new
MP4 is still needed to judge that part. No parity or retention score is claimed.


The first export of this revision exposed overlapping headings during full-
composition dissolves. Those dissolves were removed before delivery. Text now
cuts cleanly; the gold marker alone preserves positional continuity. Regression
checks cover both intermediate layouts and the measured section entry cuts.


The deposit year now changes with the second recorded phrase. During the first
phrase, the large 9.0-year benchmark is labelled 2015. At the second phrase,
2015 becomes the comparison reference and the 2025 figure counts towards 11.2.
This prevents a held 9.0 value appearing as if it were the 2025 observation.
The targeted source, layout and timing suite passes all 34 tests after this fix.
