# The Desk Reel editorial standard

## Reference and purpose

The Glasshouse reference is https://www.instagram.com/reel/DcvHmYLAycw/ and
Ruben's supplied screenshots and description of motion. Direct Instagram
playback was unavailable, so exact cut timing, vocal performance and sound

Our target is immediate understanding: what is happening, why it matters, what
it means and what the viewer should remember. More animation alone is not enough.

## The housing story

The question is why building more homes can coexist with a growing shortage.
Explain supply as homes added and demand as extra homes needed before introducing
national figures. An illustrative moving-out example shows how the same people
can form two households needing two homes.

Compare 232,000 net additions with 287,000 estimated additional need for the same
Australian period, July 2024 to December 2025. The gap grew by about 55,000 homes:
roughly 81 added for every 100 additionally needed. Gross completions and implied
demolitions remain in the source read; the Reel labels additions after demolitions
without introducing a second calculation before the main comparison.

The ending teaches a distinction: meeting new need stops the gap growing;
outpacing new need starts closing an existing gap. It does not show the 19/100
flow illustration as though it measures Australia's total accumulated shortage.
The caption follows the same logic and asks whether we are catching up, not just
whether we are building. No local price, rent or homelessness effect is inferred.

## Design direction

Retain navy and gold for recognition. Use teal consistently for need and coral
for the uncovered gap. Give the Reel freedom to use bold sans serif explanatory
headings, larger simple labels, solid tiles and fewer simultaneous numbers.
The licensed bundled DejaVu Sans Bold subset is named Desk Sans in the renderer;
other card templates retain their existing typography. Keep the original font
licence with the subset. Numbers retain aligned monospaced figures.

Show the two sides of the comparison before the chart. Keep the supply row and
scale fixed while demand arrives. Highlight the uncovered portion within demand.
Use 100 filled house tiles to make 81/100 readable on a phone. The final two
statements reuse the established supply and demand colours and highlight with
the relevant narration. The actual Council report cover, full publisher, title,
date and page support the comparison without becoming a whole source-only scene.

## Voice, timing and delivery

Keep the approved local Fable voice at speed 1.0. Use complete explanations rather
than compressed sentence fragments. This reviewed story may run up to 46 seconds;
other Reels retain their existing 32-second limit. Measured speech must remain
within that bound. Do not speed up the voice to conceal an overlong script.

Use eight scenes with at most 16 authored phrases through the existing bounded
local voice queue. Preserve 100ms before and 160ms after detectable speech when
trimming phrase edges, join with 80ms silence and use measured phrase durations
for subtitles and designated actions. This is phrase timing, not forced word
alignment. Both bars wait for their numeric phrases; the ratio retains its
bounded reveal and reading hold. Round picture timing to the 30fps frame grid.

Apply loudness normalisation to the housing mix, preserving the timing, and
encode stereo AAC at 48kHz with the video in a fast-start MP4. Inspect the actual
encoded file, including audio level and complete picture duration. Deliver the
MP4 download link because the chat's inline preview has failed for this user.

## Repeatable editorial gates

- State the story in one sentence before selecting graphics.
- Explain unfamiliar terms before presenting the main figures.
- Match each important spoken fact to a meaningful visual action.
- Keep one comparison, one geography and one period clear.
- Make the source recognisable and its full evidence reachable from the bio.
- Finish with a usable distinction or question, not another repeated number.
- Use Australian English and no em dashes in captions.
- Review the encoded MP4 for legibility, narration, timing and sound-off meaning.
- Use retention, completion, saves and replies after posting to assess results.
  A design review cannot establish parity with Glasshouse's audience performance.
